# Editorial independence and disclosure

FavRepos is an independent catalog of useful technological resources. No vendor or project is endorsed merely by inclusion.

### Information layers
- **Facts:** URLs, publisher, source release, code/license evidence.
- **Claims:** source-attributed project descriptions and performance numbers.
- **Review:** observed strengths, limits, operational requirements, reproducible tests.
- **Opinion:** personal impressions clearly labeled and dated.

A review should disclose whether the author has hands-on experience, commercial ties, free access, sponsorship, or affiliate incentives.

### Publication safety
Before public release, review for proprietary business data, patient/customer information, secrets, machine paths, credentials, logs, or internal notes. Do not publish third-party code, screenshots or media without permission or a compatible license.

### Scope
Activity and sector tags are general-purpose, not organization-specific. Reviews are context-dependent, can age quickly, and should be challenged with evidence.


### Rights and attribution
Original FavRepos editorial text and protectable editorial metadata use the scope stated in [LICENSE-CONTENT.md](../LICENSE-CONTENT.md). Original software code, scripts, and tests use the root MIT license within the scope explained by [LICENSE-SCOPE.md](../LICENSE-SCOPE.md). Publisher claims, project names, third-party text, code, models, documentation, media, and assets are not relicensed by these declarations. Keep attributed factual claims distinct from reproduced expression; do not treat a citation as permission.
